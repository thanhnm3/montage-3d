A home now costs 5 years of income. Every single dollar you earn. Not savings. Your household's entire income, for 5 years straight. Harvard tracks this. It's called the home price to income ratio. In the 1990s it sat around 3. Three years of income, long called affordable. The 2005 bubble pushed it to a record. The crash dragged it back near 3. Then prices climbed again, year after year. In 2024 the median home hit 412,500 dollars. 5 times income. A tie with the bubble. Follow for the real price of everyday life. So keep those eyes open. The house is 5 years away.
