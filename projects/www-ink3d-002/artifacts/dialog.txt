Red makes bulls angry. Everyone knows that. Everyone except the bull. Bulls can't even see red. Our eyes have three kinds of color cones. A bull's eyes have only two. So that bright red cape looks dull and muddy to him. So what makes him charge? Movement. When the MythBusters tested it, the bull charged every color the same. Then it ignored a red target standing still, and went for whatever moved. The cape is red for us. It hides the stains, and it looks dramatic to the crowd. So the bull isn't angry at red. He's angry at the wave.
