You only use 10% of your brain. Movies love that idea. Your brain doesn't. Your brain is just 2% of your body weight, but it burns about 20% of your energy. Your body would never pay that much to keep 90% switched off. Evolution would have cut it long ago. Brain scans agree. Not every part lights up at once, but over a day, almost every region gets its turn. So you don't use 10% of your brain. You use all of it, just not all at once.
